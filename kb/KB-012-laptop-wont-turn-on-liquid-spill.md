# Laptop won't turn on, or liquid was spilled on it

Use this when the laptop shows nothing when you press power, stays on a black screen, or after a spill, drop or crack.

**If liquid was spilled on the laptop:** unplug the charger, hold the power button for 10 seconds to switch it off, don't turn it back on, and don't use a hair dryer. Turn it upside down in a tent shape on a towel. **Raise a CRITICAL ticket right away** — turning it on can short the board.

## Symptoms

- Nothing happens when you press the power button
- Lights or fan come on, but the screen stays black
- The laptop got wet, was dropped, or the screen is cracked
- The laptop turns on and immediately turns off

## Quick fixes

1. Connect the {{company}} charger and wait 15 minutes — a fully drained battery needs time before it will start.
2. Check the charging light comes on. If not, try a different wall socket and charger.
3. Hold the power button for 15 seconds, release, then press it once normally.
4. If you hear the fan but see nothing, press the screen brightness-up key a few times, then try Windows + Ctrl + Shift + B (Windows) to wake the display.
5. Unplug docks, USB drives and external monitors, then try again.

## Advanced steps

1. Connect an external monitor. If the picture appears there, the laptop's own screen is likely faulty.
2. On a Mac, hold the power button for 10 seconds, release, and press again to restart it.

## Escalate when

- Any liquid spill — raise as CRITICAL and don't switch the laptop on
- The laptop was dropped or the screen is cracked
- It still won't turn on after charging for 15 minutes with a known-good charger
- You see a burning smell, smoke or swelling — stop using it and raise as CRITICAL
- You need a loaner laptop to keep working (say so in the ticket)
