# Bluetooth headset, mouse or keyboard not working

Use this when a wireless headset, mouse or keyboard won't pair, keeps disconnecting, or lags.

## Symptoms

- The device doesn't show up when you try to pair it
- It connects, then disconnects after a while
- The mouse pointer jumps or the keyboard misses keys
- A headset is connected but there's no sound or the mic isn't picked up

## Quick fixes

1. Charge the device or replace its batteries — low battery causes most lag and dropouts.
2. Turn Bluetooth off and on again on the laptop.
3. Turn the device off and on, or press its pairing button until the light flashes.
4. Remove the device and pair it again: Settings → Bluetooth & devices (Windows) or System Settings → Bluetooth (Mac) → remove, then add it back.
5. If it came with a small USB receiver, plug the receiver into a port directly on the laptop, not a dock or hub.

## Advanced steps

1. For headsets, choose the right input and output device in Sound settings and in Teams (Settings → Devices).
2. Move away from other wireless devices, and keep the device within a couple of metres of the laptop.
3. Install pending updates, then restart.

## Escalate when

- The device doesn't work on any computer (likely faulty — request a replacement)
- Bluetooth is missing from Settings on your laptop
- You need a {{company}}-approved headset, mouse or keyboard
