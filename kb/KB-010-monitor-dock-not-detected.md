# External monitor or docking station not working

Use this when your second screen shows "No signal", flickers, or the dock's keyboard, mouse or network don't work.

## Symptoms

- The monitor says "No signal" or stays black
- The display is detected but blurry, stretched or flickering
- USB devices or the wired network on the dock don't work
- The laptop doesn't charge from the dock

## Quick fixes

1. Check the monitor is switched on and set to the right input (HDMI, DisplayPort or USB-C) using the button on the monitor.
2. Unplug the dock's cable from the laptop, wait 10 seconds and plug it back in firmly.
3. Use the main USB-C/Thunderbolt port on the laptop (usually marked with a lightning or display icon).
4. Press Windows + P and choose **Extend** (Windows), or go to System Settings → Displays (Mac) to detect the screen.
5. Restart the laptop with the dock already connected.

## Advanced steps

1. Unplug the dock's power adapter for 30 seconds, then plug it back in to reset it.
2. Try a different cable or another monitor to find out which part is faulty.
3. Install updates and any dock firmware offered in {{software_centre}}, then restart.
4. If the picture is blurry, set the resolution to the one marked "Recommended" in display settings.

## Escalate when

- The monitor doesn't work with a different laptop or cable either (likely a faulty monitor)
- The dock gets very hot, smells of burning, or the laptop doesn't charge from it — stop using it and raise as HIGH
- You need a new dock, monitor or cable
