# Shared mailbox or shared calendar not showing

Use this when a team mailbox (like finance@ or support@) or a colleague's calendar doesn't appear in Outlook, or you can't send from a shared address.

## Symptoms

- A shared mailbox you were given access to isn't in Outlook
- You can't send "From" the shared address
- A colleague's or team calendar doesn't show up
- Meeting room bookings fail

## Quick fixes

1. Wait up to an hour after access is granted, then close and reopen Outlook — shared mailboxes are added automatically.
2. Check it works on the web: at outlook.office.com, click your profile picture → **Open another mailbox** and type the shared address.
3. To send from a shared address, show the **From** field (Options → Show From) and pick the shared mailbox.
4. To add a calendar, go to Calendar → **Add calendar → From directory** and search for the person or team.

## Advanced steps

1. In new Outlook, right-click **Shared with me** in the folder list → **Add shared folder or mailbox**.
2. For room bookings, add the room from the **Room Finder** in the meeting invite instead of typing its name.

## Escalate when

- You don't have access yet — raise an access request naming the mailbox and your manager
- The mailbox opens on the web but never appears in Outlook after a day
- You get "You don't have permission to send as" after access was confirmed
