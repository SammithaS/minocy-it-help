# Laptop is running slow or freezing

Use this when your laptop takes ages to open apps, freezes, or the fan runs loudly all the time.

## Symptoms

- Apps take a long time to open or show "Not responding"
- The whole laptop freezes for a few seconds at a time
- The fan is loud and the laptop feels hot
- Startup takes more than a few minutes

## Quick fixes

1. Restart the laptop (Start → Power → Restart on Windows, Apple menu → Restart on Mac). Shutting the lid only puts it to sleep, so a restart clears more.
2. Close apps and browser tabs you aren't using. Each Chrome or Edge tab can use a lot of memory, and Teams with video uses more.
3. Plug in the charger. On battery, laptops often slow themselves down to save power.
4. Check free disk space — keep at least 15% free. On Windows go to Settings → System → Storage; on Mac go to System Settings → General → Storage. Empty the Recycle Bin / Trash and clear Downloads.
5. Let pending updates finish and restart. Updates installing in the background slow everything down.

## Advanced steps

1. Find what's using the most CPU or memory: Task Manager (Ctrl + Shift + Esc) on Windows, or Activity Monitor on Mac. Close anything unexpected that is using a lot — but don't end security or {{company}} management processes.
2. Turn off apps that start automatically: Task Manager → Startup apps on Windows, or System Settings → General → Login Items on Mac.
3. Make sure the laptop isn't overheating: use it on a hard flat surface, not a bed or cushion, and keep the air vents clear.
4. Move large personal files (videos, old downloads) into OneDrive so they don't take up local disk space.

## Escalate when

- The laptop is still slow after a restart and the steps above
- Disk space is full and you can't free enough without deleting work files
- The laptop is older than 4 years, or you see disk or memory errors
- The fan runs at full speed even with nothing open, or the laptop shuts down from heat
- You notice pop-ups or programs you didn't install (treat as a possible security issue)
