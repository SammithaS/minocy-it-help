# Request access to an app or shared drive

Use this when you need access to an application (like Jira, Figma or Salesforce) or a shared drive, or you see "Access denied" for something you need for your job.

## Symptoms

- "You don't have access" or "Access denied" when opening an app or folder
- An app asks you to request a licence
- A new joiner or someone who changed teams is missing access

## Quick fixes

1. Sign out of the app and back in — access granted recently only applies after a fresh sign-in.
2. Check you're signed in with your {{company}} account (@{{email_domain}}), not a personal account.
3. Use **Request access** in the portal and pick the app. Add one line on why you need it; your manager gets an approval email.
4. Once approved, wait up to 30 minutes, then sign out and back in.

## Advanced steps

1. For shared drives, check the exact folder path with the person who shared it — you may have access to the parent folder but not the subfolder.
2. If a colleague in your team has the same access, mention their name in the request so IT can copy the right permission group.

## Escalate when

- Your request was approved more than a day ago but access still doesn't work
- You need admin or elevated rights (these always need a ticket and security review)
- Access is needed urgently for a client deliverable — say so in the request
