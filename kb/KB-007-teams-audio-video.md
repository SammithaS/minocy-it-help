# Teams calls - no sound, mic or camera not working

Use this when people can't hear or see you in Teams, you can't hear them, or calls are choppy.

## Symptoms

- Others can't hear you, or you can't hear them
- The camera shows a black screen or "Camera not found"
- Echo, robotic voice or frozen video in calls
- Teams picked the wrong headset or speakers

## Quick fixes

1. Check you aren't muted in Teams and that your headset's own mute switch is off.
2. In the meeting, click **More (…) → Settings → Device settings** and choose the right speaker, microphone and camera.
3. Unplug and replug the headset or camera, then reselect it in Teams.
4. Check camera privacy: on some laptops there's a physical shutter or a camera key (F8/F10) that turns it off.
5. Quit Teams fully (right-click its icon → Quit) and open it again.

## Advanced steps

1. Allow Teams to use your mic and camera: Settings → Privacy & security → Microphone / Camera (Windows), or System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone / Camera (Mac).
2. Close other apps that might be using the camera or mic (Zoom, browser tabs with video).
3. For choppy calls, turn off your video, move closer to the Wi-Fi access point, or use a wired connection. See the Wi-Fi guide if the connection keeps dropping.
4. Make a test call: Teams → Settings → Devices → **Make a test call**.

## Escalate when

- The camera or mic doesn't work in any app (possible driver or hardware fault)
- Problems happen only in one meeting room — report the room name (raise as HIGH if a meeting is about to start)
- Teams crashes when you join a call
- You need a {{company}}-approved headset
